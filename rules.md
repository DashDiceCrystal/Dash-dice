# Progressive Farkle — Game Rules (Crystal's variant)

## 1. Game Setup
- Players: 2–6 players (selectable).
- Each player rolls one die to determine who goes first. Highest roll starts.
- If players tie for highest, those players reroll until one is highest.

## 2. Turn Structure
Each player's turn:
1. Start with 6 dice.
2. Roll the dice.
3. Keep scoring dice.
4. Roll the remaining dice.
5. Continue rolling as long as the player wishes.
6. A player may stop and bank once they legally can bank.
- Turn ends when the player banks, or the player busts.

## 3. Roll Set Rule (Important)
- A roll set begins when the player rolls six dice.
- During the roll set, dice may be kept and rerolled.
- The roll set continues until the player busts, or all dice score (AND ROLLING).
- If all dice score: the player receives 6 new dice and a new roll set begins.
- Activation resets when a new roll set begins.

## 4. Basic Scoring
- Single 1 = 100
- Single 5 = 50

## 5. Three of a Kind (Activation)
- Three of the same number activates that face.
- Activation allows additional dice of that face to score individually during the same roll set.

| Face | 3 of a kind |
|------|-------------|
| 1 | 1000 |
| 2 | 200 |
| 3 | 300 |
| 4 | 400 |
| 5 | 500 |
| 6 | 600 |

## 6. Activated Dice Scoring
After a face activates, extra dice of that face score:
- Face × 100 per die
- Except 1s, which score 1000 each.

## 7. Four / Five of a Kind
These happen within the same roll set.

| Face | 4 of a kind | 5 of a kind |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | 4000 | 5000 |
| 2 | 800 | 1000 |
| 3 | 1200 | 1500 |
| 4 | 1600 | 2000 |
| 5 | 2000 | 2500 |
| 6 | 2400 | 3000 |

## 8. Six of a Kind
- If all six dice show the same number in one roll of six dice: **Instant Win**.

## 9. Straight
- 1 2 3 4 5 6 = 1500 points

## 10. Three Pairs
- e.g. 2 2 4 4 6 6 = 1000 points

## 11. Doubles Rule
- If only 2 dice remain and they are the same number (e.g. 4 4): AND ROLLING — the player receives 6 new dice.

## 12. Bust
- A player busts when a roll produces no scoring dice.
- Turn ends, turn score = 0.

## 13. Banking Rules
To bank points:
- Player must have at least 1000 points in the turn.
- Player must be down to the allowed number of dice.
- Two rule options:
  - House Rule: bank with 1 die remaining
  - Classic Rule: bank with 2 dice remaining
- Even if the player can bank, they may continue rolling.

## 14. Entering the Scoreboard
- A player must reach 1000 points in one turn before they can bank their first score.

## 15. Final Round
- When a player reaches 12,000 or more, the final round begins.
- All remaining players get one final full turn.
- Players must beat the leader's score by 50 or more to take the lead.
- The highest score after the table finishes wins.

## App-level requirements (from Crystal)
- Progressive scoring (this ruleset).
- Online multiplayer: play against each other live.
- Persistent scores (accounts).
- Level-up system: the more games played, the better dice unlocked.
