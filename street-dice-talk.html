📖 Street Dice 12,000 — Rules

🎯 Goal: First to 12,000 wins. When someone reaches 12,000+, everyone else gets one last turn — and must beat the leader by 50 or more to take the lead.

🎲 Your turn: Roll 6 dice. Keep any scoring dice, then roll what is left. Keep going as long as you like — or bank when the rules let you. A roll set starts with 6 fresh dice and ends when you bust or all dice score.

💯 Scoring: A single 1 = 100. A single 5 = 50. Three of a kind kept in one selection: 1s 1000, 2s 200, 3s 300, 4s 400, 5s 500, 6s 600. Four of a kind = face × 400 (1s 4000). Five of a kind = face × 500 (1s 5000).

🔓 Activation: Keep 3 of one face in one selection and that face is ACTIVATED for the rest of the roll set. After that, every die of that face scores on its own: face × 100 each, 1s 1000 each — even a later set of 3 scores the activated rate, not the table again. Piling up 3 across different rolls does NOT activate. A fresh set of 6 dice clears activation.

🎲🎲 Doubles — roll only: If you roll your last 2 dice in a roll set and they match, you get 6 fresh dice (AND ROLLING). A pair just sitting there after a keep is NOT doubles — you have to roll them.

🏦 Banking — all four must be true: (1) you have kept dice, (2) your turn total is 1000 or more, (3) you are down to 1 die left, (4) NOT every die scored. If every die scored, that is AND ROLLING — you must roll the fresh 6, you cannot bank. (Rooms can also use the classic 2-dice rule — the room setting applies.)

💥 Bust: A roll with no scoring dice ends your turn and your turn score becomes 0.

🌈 Combos: Straight 1-2-3-4-5-6 = 1500. Three pairs (like 2-2-4-4-6-6) = 1000.

👑 Instant win: Six of a kind in one roll of six dice wins the game on the spot.
